Privacy Policy

How FITFUEL handles information used by the website.

1. Information We Collect

FITFUEL may use information that you enter into the fitness planner, such as your name, age, gender, height, weight, fitness goal, workout days and diet preference. Progress entries may also include weight and body measurements.

2. Local Storage

The current FITFUEL version stores plan information, selected meals, completed exercises, progress history and the product shortlist in your browser using local storage. This means the information is primarily stored on the device and browser where you use FITFUEL.

3. Third-Party Services

FITFUEL may load content or services from third parties, including YouTube, Spotify, jsDelivr and MuscleBlaze. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Camera Feature

The optional exercise-repetition tracker requests camera permission only when you choose to enable it. Camera access can be denied through your browser. Do not use the feature if you are uncomfortable granting camera access.

5. Email Contact

If you contact us at fitwithme413@gmail.com, we may receive the information you include in your message so that we can respond to your request.

6. Data Deletion

You can clear information stored locally by using the reset and clear-history controls provided by FITFUEL or by clearing the site's data in your browser.

7. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as FITFUEL adds or changes features. Updated versions will be published on this website.
